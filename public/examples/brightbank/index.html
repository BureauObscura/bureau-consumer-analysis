Fictional brand · synthetic outcomes

Brightbank soda

An orange-and-vanilla cane-sugar soda. One Instagram creative, a $28.80 twelve-pack, eight purchase journeys and six price points.

The study tests product information, shipping, discounts and checkout requirements. Every journey evaluates the same 10 million synthetic profiles.
